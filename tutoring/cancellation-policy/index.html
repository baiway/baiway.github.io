Cancellation & rescheduling policy

This policy is really aimed at repeated last-minute cancellations, not the occasional mishap. I apply it with common sense. If something serious or unavoidable has happened, just talk to me — I'm a reasonable person.

Giving notice

A discretionary charge may be applied for no-shows and late-notice cancellations. The fee structure is detailed in the table below.

Notice given Fee
More than 24 hours Free of charge
Less than 24 hours 50% of lesson fee
No-show 100% of lesson fee

To make this system as fair as possible:

  • Students get one 'free pass' every 12 weeks. Things happen and I'm willing to be flexible. Your first late cancellation in any 12-week period (roughly equal to a school term length) will not be charged. Subsequent cancellations will be subject to a discretionary charge though (outlined in the table above).
  • Rescheduling within the same week is free. If you move the lesson to another time in the same week rather than cancelling it, there's no charge, even inside the 24-hour window. I'd always rather keep the teaching going than charge for a missed slot.

Getting organised

You can view our scheduled lessons by going to calendar.google.com. A screenshot of my Google Calendar is shown below, for example. Yours will of course look different (you will only see your lessons with me and other events you add yourself — you will not see my lessons with other students).

Make sure you're signed into the Google Account associated with the email address you gave me though, otherwise events will not be visible. If you or your child have never used Google Calendar before, I'm more than happy to walk you or them through it.
